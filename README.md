## Typography Matters: Analysis and Professional Redesign

### Introduction

Typography plays a critical role in how users perceive credibility, professionalism, and usability within a website, especially in fields that rely on trust and clarity. In this assignment, you will analyze a poorly designed webpage to understand how font choices, hierarchy, and readability can either support or undermine a site’s purpose and audience. Developing strong typography skills is essential in this course because typography directly impacts user experience and accessibility in web design. Professionally and personally, learning to make intentional typography decisions helps you communicate information clearly and present your work with confidence and credibility.
